01 — Statement

Artist Statement

My artistic practice is a dialogue at the intersection of contemporary painting and traditional felt, aimed at interpreting the cultural code of the Great Steppe. I explore archaic cultural layers through plastic gestures, textures, and rhythms, translating mythological concepts into the language of abstraction. In my paintings (Oblivion, Elegy), I employ a rich palette and loose layering of pigments. For me, it is essential that the artistic image results from deep internal experience rather than mere observation, evolving into a meditative statement on continuity and feminine strength.
02 — Works

Selected Works

Oblivion (2025)

A visual meditation on the fluidity of time, where the human silhouette dissolves into a deep blue space of memory.

Elegy (2025)

This piece investigates archetypal connections between humanity and nature, using neo-figurative symbolism to depict the harmonious coexistence of the past and the present.

Steppe Melody (2026, Felt)

A large-scale felt panel where the silhouettes of horses emerge from the material through rhythm and texture, embodying the dynamic spirit and breath of the Great Steppe.
